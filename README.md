# WatchParty

WatchParty is a tiny private movie-room experience built with Three.js. Instead of just sending a link and saying "press play at the same time", it gives friends a little virtual theatre to walk into, sit down, react, chat, and share a screen together.

Think of it as the cozy middle ground between a video call and a cinema hall: you enter with your name, get a room key, choose a seat, and watch the shared screen from inside a 3D theatre scene.

## What It Does

- Creates or joins a private room with a simple room key
- Lets guests enter with their own display name
- Shows a 3D theatre scene made with Three.js
- Lets you click seats and move into a seated camera view
- Supports screen sharing on the theatre screen
- Includes quick reactions like Tomato, LOL, Wow, and Clap
- Has a small chat panel for live comments

## Why It Exists

Watching something together online can feel flat when everyone is just sitting in separate tabs. WatchParty adds a bit of place and personality back into it. The goal is not to be a giant streaming platform. The goal is to make a shared movie night feel more like an event.
